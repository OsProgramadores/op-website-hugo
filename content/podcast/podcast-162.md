In this episode of the OsProgramadores Podcast, Marcelo sits down with Tim O'Reilly, founder and CEO of O'Reilly Media, to discuss the evolution of technology, open source, artificial intelligence, software engineering, and the responsibility of technologists in shaping the future.

Widely recognised as one of the most influential voices in the technology industry, Tim shares lessons from decades of observing—and helping shape—the software ecosystem, from the early days of the Internet to today's AI revolution.



----------------------------------------




👨‍💻 ABOUT THE GUEST

Tim O'Reilly is the Founder and CEO of O'Reilly Media, one of the world's most respected technology publishing and learning companies.

For more than four decades, Tim has helped developers, entrepreneurs, and technology leaders understand emerging technologies before they become mainstream.

He is widely credited with popularising and advancing several concepts that have shaped the modern technology landscape, including:

🌐 Web 2.0

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

📚 Open knowledge and continuous learning

🛠️ Open source software

☁️ Cloud computing

Throughout his career, Tim has championed the idea that technology should create more opportunities for society rather than simply automate existing work.

As the founder of O'Reilly Media, he transformed technical publishing through the iconic animal-covered books that have educated generations of software engineers around the world.

Beyond publishing, Tim has organised and supported influential technology conferences and communities that have brought together innovators from across the industry.

He is also the author of several books and essays exploring topics such as:

📖 Artificial Intelligence

📖 The future of work

📖 Platform economics

📖 The Internet economy

📖 Government as a Platform

Tim is recognised not only for identifying important technological trends, but also for asking deeper questions about how technology can serve humanity.
